Claim fence

This page does not say that a real dendritic branch is an arbitrary software program.

The narrower result is:

passive equality ≠ operator equality
if the passive trajectory never excites the direction on which the operators differ.

Yang et al. (2016) and Onasch et al. (2026) are biological motivation for branch-local nonlinear processing and contextual gating. The probe-identification mechanism is ours to test, not something those papers establish.
