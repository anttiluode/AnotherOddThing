What q means

Icorrect = Iclosed − (1−q)·relief
Iwrong = Iclosed − q·relief

q=0: plasticity relief is aligned with expression.

q=0.5: correct and wrong compartments receive equal intermediate inhibition.

q=1: relief has moved fully to the wrong compartment.

Claim fence

The continuous attacker does not erase the difference.

In this fixed toy world, categorical local plasticity makes the learned structure lose alignment earlier and more sharply under the same continuously moved inhibitory set-point.

This is not evidence for a biological critical point, not a claim that real calcium follows this toy rule, and not a universal argument for quantized plasticity. The continuous rule is an adversarial control, not a biological model.
